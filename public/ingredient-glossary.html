Ingredient Glossary

Explore skincare ingredients and their benefits in our interactive glossary.

Vitamin C

Vitamin C is a potent antioxidant that helps brighten the skin and reduce the appearance of dark spots and hyperpigmentation.

Retinol

Retinol, a form of Vitamin A, is commonly used to fight signs of aging by reducing wrinkles and improving skin texture.

Hyaluronic Acid

Hyaluronic Acid is a powerful hydrating ingredient that can attract and retain moisture in the skin, making it appear plumper and more hydrated.

Niacinamide

Niacinamide, or Vitamin B3, is known for its anti-inflammatory properties, helping to calm the skin and reduce redness, acne, and irritation.

Salicylic Acid

Salicylic Acid is a beta hydroxy acid (BHA) that penetrates deep into the pores to exfoliate and clear acne-causing debris.
