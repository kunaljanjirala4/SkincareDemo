Skincare 101

Your beginner-friendly guide to understanding your skin and building a routine that actually works.

Step 1: Get to Know Your Skin

The first and most important step in any skincare journey is identifying your skin type. This determines which products will be your best friends and which you should avoid.

Oily Skin

Looks shiny or greasy, especially in the T-zone (forehead, nose, chin). Pores may appear enlarged, and you might be prone to breakouts.

Dry Skin

Feels tight, especially after cleansing. May have flaky patches, redness, or a rough texture. Pores are typically small.

Combination Skin

The most common skin type. Features an oily T-zone with normal or dry cheeks. Requires balancing different needs.

Sensitive Skin

Reacts easily to new products. Often experiences stinging, burning, or redness. Requires gentle, soothing ingredients.

Step 2: Build Your Core Routine

You don't need a 10-step routine to start. Master these three non-negotiable steps first.

STEP 01

Cleanse

Wash your face morning and night to remove makeup, dirt, oil, and impurities. This creates a clean canvas for other products to work effectively. Use a gentle cleanser that doesn't strip your skin.

STEP 02

Moisturize

Hydrating your skin is crucial for all skin types (even oily!). A good moisturizer helps repair your skin's barrier, prevents water loss, and keeps your skin soft and plump.

STEP 03 (AM ONLY)

Protect with SPF

Sunscreen is the single most effective anti-aging product you can use. Protect your skin from UV damage every single day, rain or shine, to prevent wrinkles, dark spots, and skin cancer.
